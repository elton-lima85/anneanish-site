About the Candidate

Community leader.
Reformist.

"Having survived a life-threatening health challenge, Anne considers this her second life — one she has chosen to dedicate fully in service of the people."

Anne Anish is a dedicated community leader, reformist, and passionate advocate for people-centric development. With years of active involvement in local initiatives, she has consistently stood for transparency, accountability, and inclusivity in governance.

Guided by deep faith and a sense of purpose, she embraces servant leadership — where true leadership means listening, caring, and working alongside the community rather than above it.

Education is at the heart of her vision. She firmly believes that every child deserves access to quality education, and tirelessly works so that no child is left behind.

Anne believes in transparent, accountable governance with regular ward meetings and public dashboards — because good governance means being answerable to the people, always.

On the Ground

Work that speaks
for itself

Real action taken for real people in the ward.

14 July 2025
AAP’s Follow-up Gets New Teacher for Bopodi School
Due to Aam Aadmi Party’s consistent follow-up, Patasibai Chhajed E-Learning English Medium School in Bopodi received a new teacher after facing shortages since last year. 40 nursery students had been promoted to Class 1, but lack of teachers forced 25 parents to withdraw admissions. Anne and AAP leaders raised the issue with PMC officials — and with a new teacher appointed, several parents are now re-enrolling their children.
13 July 2025
Pot the Potholes 🌿
Every pothole in the ward was turned into a pot of green by planting saplings — a powerful symbolic step to demand safe roads and remind authorities of their responsibility to our community.
November 2023
A Tale of Hope — Saving a Life
When a young woman (Pinky, name changed) was fighting for her life, Anne mobilised AAP members, doctors, and community leaders overnight. She coordinated a transfer to Ruby Hall Hospital, ensured admission without an upfront deposit, and spearheaded a donation drive raising over ₹6.5 lakhs — qualifying the patient for the Indigent Patient Fund and saving the family from financial ruin.
